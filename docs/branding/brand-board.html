Doomscroll Ad Free — three brand directions

Each option defines the icon, palette, type voice, and personality. In-app UI uses Android’s Material You (wallpaper colors); the brand lives in the icon, store listing, widget, and marketing.

Ink & Signal

“Your feeds. Zero ads.”

Icon concept

Ink rounded-square with three “feed lines” — one signal-orange (the story you care about). Legible at 24 px, recognizable on any wallpaper, works as monochrome.

Why it works
  • Matches the app’s restrained, text-first UI
  • Ages well — no trend risk for a long-lived open-source project
  • Newspaper-of-record energy: “we respect your attention”
Watch out

Less playful; depends on typography to feel distinctive.

Icon concept

A friendly “doom cloud” character — cozy, weird, memorable — with a coral “no-ads” badge. Plus a lime accent for highlights and stickers.

Why it works
  • Instantly memorable; great for store screenshots and word-of-mouth
  • Owns the doomscroll irony the name already sells
  • Mascot can grow into onboarding, empty states, and merch
Watch out

Humor dates; mascot needs consistent illustration. Some users may want “serious utility”.

Broadcast

“Your media page, minus the noise.”

Icon concept

Electric indigo with a fanned “stack of story cards” and a signal-yellow highlight line — literally the feed, reimagined as your channel.

Why it works
  • Most aligned with “media page replacement” positioning
  • High contrast pops on the home screen and in store listings
  • Card motif extends naturally to widgets and screenshots
Watch out

Indigo + candy yellow can read as “generic tech startup”; needs disciplined typography.

Chosen: Friendly Doom (Nov 15, 2026 — switched after live review)

The doom-cloud mascot makes the app memorable and gives the project a personality contributors can build on. Ink & Signal remains a fallback if the playful direction ever needs to be dialed back; Broadcast remains the strongest choice for a hard "media channel" positioning.

Decided: launcher label is "Doomscroll" (full name "Doomscroll Ad Free" kept for store/README), and the app icon uses the 66dp-safe-zone doom cloud on Midnight Plum.
